ASI Elections Vote Campaign

A campaign I designed as a Graphic Designer for Associated Students, Inc. at CSUSB to get students voting in the 2026 ASI Elections. Each day of voting had its own post and treat, free for the first 200 students who showed proof of voting: 985 Coffee, Dubai Strawberry cups and Nothing Bundt Cakes Bundtinis.

When the election went to a run off, I extended the campaign for two more days of voting, Crumbl cookies on April 21 and donuts on April 22, swapping the VOTE pattern for a repeating RUN OFF VOTING pattern so the new round felt connected but new.

I designed everything in Adobe Illustrator, giving each day its own color palette, and adapted each design to every format the campaign needed: Instagram feed posts (1080 × 1440 px) and stories (1080 × 1920 px), SMSU screens, Coyote Connection graphics, 11 × 17 in posters, 8.5 × 11 in flyers and 4.25 × 5.5 in handouts.
